Power Rankings

Who's actually good? Record counts 50%, points scored 30% and roster strength 20%. Enter your Sleeper league ID and press Run.

Takes a few seconds: it reads every week's scores and Sleeper's projections for the rest of the season.

How the score works. Each team gets three numbers: win percentage, total points scored, and roster strength. Each one is turned into a z-score (how far above or below the league average, in standard deviations). The z-scores are weighted 50% record, 30% points and 20% roster, then scaled to a 0–100 score where 50 is average: score = (weighted z + 2.5) ÷ 5 × 100.
Roster strength is the best legal starting lineup each team can field, averaged over the next four weeks, using your league's scoring. Each player's weekly number is 50% his points per game so far and 50% Sleeper's projected points per game. A player on bye that week, or projected out, counts as zero, Questionable players count 78% for the coming game and Doubtful 10%. Empty spots are filled at waiver-wire level.
Movement compares with last week's rankings, rebuilt from last week's record and points with the same roster score.
